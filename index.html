Privacy Policy

Cool Snail — Block Short Videos

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Cool Snail helps you stop scrolling short video. This page explains exactly what the app can see, what it does with it, and what leaves your phone.

The short version: nothing you do inside other apps ever leaves your device. There are no ads, no trackers and no analytics.

The accessibility permission

Cool Snail uses Android's Accessibility Service. This is the only way Android allows an app to know that you are currently inside an infinite video feed in another app, so it is required for the app to work at all.

Here is precisely what that means:

  • The app reads which screen is currently open in Instagram, TikTok and YouTube — it looks for the identifiers of the Reels, TikTok and Shorts players.
  • It does not read your messages, your passwords, what you type, your photos, or the content of the videos.
  • All of this happens on your device, in memory. Nothing is recorded, stored or transmitted anywhere.
  • The only action it performs is closing that video feed.
  • You grant this permission yourself, and you can revoke it at any time from Android Settings › Accessibility.

What is stored on your phone

The app keeps a small counter of how many times it has closed a feed, so it can show you your own numbers. This stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. It is never sent anywhere.

What does leave your phone

Only what is needed to sell and restore a subscription:

  • Google Play processes your payment. Cool Snail never sees your card details.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions on our behalf and receives your purchase status together with an anonymous identifier it generates. It does not receive your name, your email or anything you do inside other apps.

You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.
